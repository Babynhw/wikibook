The rapid integration of sophisticated generative models into educational workflows presents a dual-edged sword. On one hand, these tools democratize access to personalized tutoring and ideation assistance; on the other, they fundamentally challenge traditional metrics of original student work.

Initial studies suggest a growing reliance on these systems for initial draft generation. It is crucial to examine the pedagogical shifts required to evaluate critical thinking rather than mere output generation.

Methodology Overview

This research adopts a mixed-methods approach, analyzing textual submissions from a cohort of 500 undergraduate students across multiple disciplines before and after the widespread availability of specific AI tools.
