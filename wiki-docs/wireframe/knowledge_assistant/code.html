What is the consensus on the long-term cognitive effects of spaced repetition in language acquisition compared to massed practice?

psychology
Folio AI

The consensus across the analyzed literature strongly favors spaced repetition over massed practice for long-term cognitive retention in language acquisition.

Several meta-analyses indicate that the "spacing effect" allows for greater neural consolidation during rest periods between sessions . Massed practice (cramming), while effective for immediate recall, shows significant decay curves over a 30-day period .

Furthermore, recent neuroimaging studies suggest that spaced retrieval practice actively strengthens hippocampal pathways, leading to more resilient semantic networks compared to the superficial encoding typical of massed practice .
